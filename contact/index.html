What to bring

Bring the messy version.

Nothing needs to be tidy. Rough notes are fine, and so is showing up with none of this.

useful to have
  • The software you already pay for. Email, calendar, CRM, scheduling, phone system, accounting. The fastest wins are often AI features already inside them.
  • The one or two jobs that eat the week. The inbox, scheduling, follow-ups, the same questions asked twice.
  • Who owns your accounts. Which Google or Microsoft account holds company email and calendar, and who can grant access.
  • Anything you've tried with AI. What worked, what didn't, and any launch you've been wondering about.
  • Your rules on client data. Confidentiality, HIPAA or anything else we should know before we suggest a tool.
