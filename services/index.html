Questions

Before you book.

All questions

Do we have to become members?

No. Every step works on its own. Most businesses start with a Head Start, then decide what's next once they have the plan.

Why do the prices say "from"?

The final price depends on the size of your team and how many systems a tool needs to connect to. We confirm the price in writing before any work starts.

Why pay for setup if the tool is self-serve?

Switching a tool on is the easy part. We set it up for how your business works, with the least access it needs, test it with a few people first, and train the team. You also keep a one-page guide to how you use it.

What does "day one" mean?

The first day a tool is available to your account. Rollouts are often staggered by plan, region or company size. We prepare before that day so setup can start right away.

Is there a minimum commitment?

Only for the membership. First Quarter has a three-month minimum, then runs month to month. Head Start, Day One Setup and Rollout Sessions are one-time.

What won't you do?

We won't train your team on tools they can't access, run generic "Intro to AI" seminars, or recommend a tool just because it's new.
