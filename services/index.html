Switching it on is the easy part.

Connecting a new assistant to your inbox takes a minute. Setting it up for how your business works, testing it safely and teaching the team is the rest of the job. That's the part we do.

  • Accounts and permissions set up with the least access the tool needs.
  • Connected to the systems it should touch: email, calendar, docs, CRM.
  • Instructions and settings written for how your business works.
  • Approval rules you set: what it must never send without a person checking.
  • A limited test with 1 to 3 people before anyone else uses it.
  • One live training session for the team.
  • A one-page "how we use this here" guide.

Level 2b · training

Rollout Session

Quoted · included in First Quarter

For teams whose tools are set up but barely used. Live training on your own work, with your own examples.

Ask about a session
  • 90 to 180 minutes, live, with your team.
  • Built around each role, so people practice the parts of the tool they'll use.
  • Real tasks from your business, brought by the team.
  • A prompt and workflow sheet everyone keeps.

Our rule: we only train on tools your team can actually use. No generic "Intro to AI" seminars.

All questions
Do we have to become members?
No. Every step works on its own. Most businesses start with a Head Start, then decide what's next once they have the plan.
Why do the prices say "from"?
The final price depends on the size of your team and how many systems a tool needs to connect to. We confirm the price in writing before any work starts.
Why pay for setup if the tool is self-serve?
Switching a tool on is the easy part. We set it up for how your business works, with the least access it needs, test it with a few people first, and train the team. You also keep a one-page guide to how you use it.
What does "day one" mean?
The first day a tool is available to your account. Rollouts are often staggered by plan, region or company size. We prepare before that day so setup can start right away.
Is there a minimum commitment?
Only for the membership. First Quarter has a three-month minimum, then runs month to month. Head Start, Day One Setup and Rollout Sessions are one-time.
What won't you do?
We won't train your team on tools they can't access, run generic "Intro to AI" seminars, or recommend a tool just because it's new.
