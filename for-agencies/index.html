Questions

Before you call.

The questions partners ask first. Anything else, bring it to the call.

What does it cost?

Partner pricing is wholesale and quoted per engagement. You set your own price to your client. We don't publish partner rates.

Is it fully white-label?

Setups can be. We work under your brand, or join calls as part of your team. The Signals feed is co-branded as Signals by QuarterSmart, so the source of each verdict stays clear.

Who owns the client?

You do. The contract and the relationship stay with you, and your client owns every account we set up.

What if a launch isn't worth it for a client?

We'll say so. Wait and Ignore are common verdicts. We'd rather tell you that than set up a tool nobody uses.

Are you affiliated with OpenAI, Anthropic, Google or Microsoft?

No. QuarterSmart is independent and sets up tools from many vendors.
