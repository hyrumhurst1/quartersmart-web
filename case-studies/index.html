Questions

About the proof.

Every number on this page links to how it was measured. If something isn't answered here, ask us on a call.

How did you measure the 65% and 36%?

We took a baseline before we built anything. For the property management client, that meant tracking onboarding time, repeat questions and SOP-related mistakes across seven roles, then comparing the same measures after the system went live. Results vary with team size and how consistent the process was before we started.

Can I talk to a client before signing?

Once we've scoped a fit, we can ask a past client whether they're willing to talk with you. The property management client approved sharing their story here without their name.

Why do some projects say they were built in another role?

Because they were. Hyrum built the Learning Journey AI platform and courses while working with that company, and the Skytech voice agent while employed there. We show them because they're real work, and we label them so you can weigh them properly.

Where are the day-one setup case studies?

Our first day-one setups are underway. Each write-up will give the date the tool became available to the client, when setup started, and a before-and-after number agreed at kickoff.

My business looks nothing like property management. Does this apply?

Usually. The pattern is the same in any business that runs on repeatable processes: set the tool up properly, train the people who use it, then automate what's left. The n8n templates on this page cover video production, lead capture, client onboarding and property operations.
