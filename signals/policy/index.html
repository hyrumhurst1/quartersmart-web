How we source Signals.

Sourcing

Public information only.

Everything in Signals was public before we wrote about it. We post as fast as we can verify, label how sure we are, and update the post when the facts change.

sourcing rules
  1. Public sources only.Published reporting, public product screens and public posts. If it isn't public, we don't cover it.
  2. Nothing confidential.We don't solicit, accept or publish confidential documents, credentials, private source code, or anything obtained by breaking a terms of service.
  3. Unconfirmed until confirmed.A reported item keeps the label "Reported · unconfirmed" until the company confirms or denies it.
  4. Sources on every post.Primary sources first, meaning the company's own post or the original author, then the reporting.
  5. Links to the original.We link to screenshots and posts where they were first published. We don't copy other people's screenshots onto our site.
  6. Corrections with timestamps.Every correction is logged at the bottom of the post with the time, marked "Correction."

Labels and verdicts

How sure we are. What to do about it.

Every Signal carries one status label and ends in one verdict. The label says how sure we are. The verdict says what a typical business should do this week.

status labels
Reported
Publicly reported, not confirmed by the company. Always marked "Reported · unconfirmed."
Confirmed
Announced by the company, not yet available to most accounts.
Available
You can use it now, on the plans listed.
Early access
A waitlist, beta or preview is open.
Quiet update
A change inside a tool you may already pay for, often only in a changelog.
Event
A launch event we're covering.
verdicts
Act now
Worth doing this week for most businesses it applies to.
Prepare
Not ready yet, but a little groundwork now saves time later.
Wait
Promising, not ready. We'll tell you when that changes.
Ignore
Noise for a working business. Skip it.

Wait and Ignore are common verdicts. That's the point.

Corrections and independence

When we're wrong, and who pays us.

corrections

Spotted a mistake?

Email hyrum@quartersmart.com with the link and what you think is wrong. If we got it wrong, we fix the post, add a timestamped correction to its update log, and record the outcome on the Signals Log.

Reported items stay on the Log whether they turn out right or wrong, so you can check our record.
