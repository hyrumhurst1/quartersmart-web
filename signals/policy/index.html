Where it comes from.

We post as fast as we can verify, label how sure we are, and update the post when the facts change.

Where we look

Vendors
Official blogs, changelogs and release notes
Your tools
Changelogs of the business software you already pay for
Press
Credible tech reporting
People
Public posts from the companies' own staff
  1. Unconfirmed until confirmed.A reported item keeps the label "Reported · unconfirmed" until the company confirms or denies it.
  2. Sources on every post.Primary sources first, meaning the company's own post or the original author, then the reporting.
  3. Corrections with timestamps.Every correction is logged at the bottom of the post with the time, marked "Correction."

How sure we are. What to do about it.

Every Signal carries one status label, and every news Signal ends in one verdict. The label says how sure we are. The verdict says what a typical business should do this week.

Status labels

Reported
Publicly reported, not confirmed by the company. Always marked "Reported · unconfirmed."
Confirmed
Announced by the company, not yet available to most accounts.
Available
You can use it now, on the plans listed.
Early access
A waitlist, beta or preview is open.
Quiet update
A change inside a tool you may already pay for, often only in a changelog.
Event
A launch event we're covering.
Insight
Our own analysis or a how-to, not news about a single launch. Nothing to confirm.
Weekly roundup
A digest of the week's items.

Inside a post, single claims can also be marked Speculation (a guess, with no report behind it) or Unknown (nobody has said yet).

Verdicts

Act now
Worth doing this week for most businesses it applies to.
Prepare
Not ready yet, but a little groundwork now saves time later.
Wait
Promising, not ready. We'll tell you when that changes.
Ignore
Noise for a working business. Skip it.

Insight posts and weekly roundups explain a trend rather than one launch, so they carry no verdict.

Corrections and fine print.

Spotted a mistake?

Email hyrum@quartersmart.com with the link and what you think is wrong. If we got it wrong, we fix the post, add a timestamped correction to its update log, and record the outcome on the Signals Log.

Reported items stay on the Log whether they turn out right or wrong.
