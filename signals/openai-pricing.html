OpenAI's pricing in spring 2026: what GPT-5.4 costs and which plan you need

OpenAI released GPT-5.4 in March. Here is which ChatGPT plans include it, what it costs on the API, and whether your team needs to move up to Pro.

The short version

  • OpenAI released GPT-5.4 on March 5, 2026, with smaller mini and nano versions on March 17.
  • In ChatGPT it came with the paid plans, starting with Plus at $20 a month. Pro, at $200 a month, is for people who hit the Plus limits every week.
  • On the API it listed at $2.50 per million input tokens and $15 per million output tokens. Check OpenAI's pricing page before you budget, because these numbers change.
  • For most teams, Plus is the right starting point. Verdict: Wait on Pro.

What GPT-5.4 is

GPT-5.4 is the model OpenAI released on March 5, 2026, in ChatGPT, the API and Codex. In ChatGPT it appears as GPT-5.4 Thinking, meant for work that takes more than one pass: long documents, multi-step reasoning and tasks that use tools. Smaller mini and nano versions followed on March 17.

Which ChatGPT plan you need

GPT-5.4 Thinking came with ChatGPT's paid plans from launch: Plus, Pro, Business and Enterprise. It was not on the free plan.

Plus, at $20 a month, is where most people should start. Pro, at $200 a month, raises the usage limits and adds GPT-5.4 Pro, a slower and more thorough version. It makes sense for someone who runs into the Plus limits every week, not as a first step.

What it costs on the API

If your team builds on OpenAI's API, GPT-5.4 listed at $2.50 per million input tokens and $15 per million output tokens at launch, with cheaper rates for cached input. The mini and nano versions cost less. Prices change, so check OpenAI's API pricing page before you budget.

What to do

Verdict: Wait. If your team already pays for Plus, you already have GPT-5.4. Hold off on Pro until someone hits the Plus limits every week, and write down which tasks those are, so you know what the upgrade buys.
