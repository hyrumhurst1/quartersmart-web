ChatGPT's mobile app can now draft, summarize and build by voice

OpenAI brought its agent-style work features to the phone, driven by voice. If your team already pays for Plus or Pro, one small test this week will tell you whether it earns a place in the day.

By Hyrum HurstPosted Sep 27, 2026Announced Sep 23, 2026

Book a 20-minute callWhat to do this week

The short version

  • On September 23, OpenAI announced voice-based work features for the ChatGPT mobile app.
  • Plus and Pro users get the Work tab on their phone: create a document, draft an email or summarize Slack messages, by voice or text.
  • Free and Go users get plugins and connected apps on mobile.
  • Work started on the phone can be picked up on the desktop.

What changed

Until now, the agent-style features in ChatGPT were something you used at a desk. The mobile app now carries the same Work tab for Plus and Pro subscribers, and you can drive it by talking. OpenAI also says voice conversations now return richer text, and that switching between voice and typing is easier.

Why it matters for a small business

Owners and field staff do a lot of their communicating from the truck, the job site or between appointments. Being able to say "draft a follow-up to this morning's quote, friendly, mention the Thursday opening" and find it waiting on the desktop is a real time saver, if the draft is good enough to send with light edits.

Who this is for, and who can skip it

Try it if your team is already on ChatGPT Plus or Pro and does client email or quoting away from a desk. Skip it for now if your team is not on a paid ChatGPT plan, or if the work involves client data your policies keep out of ChatGPT.

What to do this week

  • Pick one person who writes a lot of email from their phone.
  • Have them voice-draft three real follow-ups and finish them on the desktop.
  • Count the edits. If each draft needed only light changes, it's worth rolling out. If not, wait for the next update.
