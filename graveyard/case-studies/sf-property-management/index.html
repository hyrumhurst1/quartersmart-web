Onboarding and enablement for a San Francisco property management company

We built an onboarding and enablement system for the client across seven roles: 39 training videos, AI-graded quizzes and certification. Measured against a baseline taken before launch, onboarding time and repeat questions fell 65%. SOP-related mistakes fell 36%.

By QuarterSmartAnonymized with the client's approval

Built for the client. This system belongs to the property management company and runs under their brand. We designed and built it for them.

Context

The client is a San Francisco property management company with a $500M+ portfolio. Its day-to-day work runs on written SOPs, spread across seven operational roles.

The problem

When someone made a mistake, they were handed the SOP and told to rewrite it by hand, so the lesson would stick. It was memorable. It didn't scale, and it didn't stop the next person from making the same mistake.

Two other costs were harder to see: how long it took a new hire to get up to speed, and how often the same questions came back to the people who already knew the answers.

What we built

An onboarding and enablement system, built for the client and run under their brand:

  • 39 training videos across seven roles. Organized by role, so each person learns the processes for their job.
  • AI-automated course and video creation. Course material and video are produced from the company's own SOPs.
  • AI-graded quizzes. Claude grades each quiz. Passing one opens the next step.
  • Certification by role. With compliance tracking, so owners know who is certified for what.
  • An owner dashboard. Who has completed what, and where people struggle.
  • One-click review. Anyone can pull up a process in a click instead of reading a document.

How we measured it

We took a baseline before we built anything. Across the seven roles, we tracked three things: onboarding time, repeat questions and SOP-related mistakes. After the system went live, we compared the same measures against that baseline.

We agree on the metrics with the client up front, so the result is something they can check.

What changed for the team

  • No more rewriting SOPs by hand. The system replaced that habit. The process behind a mistake is one click away.
  • New hires learn by role. Video and AI-graded quizzes for each of the seven roles, with a certification at the end.
  • Fewer repeat questions. The people who already know the answers get asked the same thing less often.
  • Owners can see it. Who has completed what, and where people struggle, on one dashboard.
Results vary. These numbers come from one client. They depend on team size and how consistently the process was followed before we started.
