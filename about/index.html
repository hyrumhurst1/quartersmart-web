What we do

The job nobody on your payroll has.

New models, agents and features ship every week, and many of them land quietly inside software you already pay for. Keeping up is a real job. In a business of 10 to 250 people, it usually belongs to nobody.

That's the job we do. We read the launches, the early-access programs, the product updates and the credible reports. We decide which ones matter to a business like yours and give each one a verdict. When a tool is worth it, we set it up in your accounts and train the people who'll use it.

We call that early adoption: using a new tool while it still gives you a head start, and skipping the ones that don't earn the time.

Why the name

Why "Quarter."

Every shift in AI has an early group. They try the new tool first, learn it while there's still room to learn, and get the time back sooner. Think of it as the first quarter of the market to move.

QuarterSmart exists to help you move with that group on purpose, and only when the move is worth making. The second half matters as much as the first. Plenty of launches aren't worth your time, and we'll tell you which ones.

The mark says the same thing: four quarters, with the first one lit.

Independence

Independent, on purpose.

QuarterSmart is not affiliated with or endorsed by OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Microsoft or any other AI vendor. We set up tools from many of them, and we name their products only to describe them.

That's what lets us say "wait" and "ignore" out loud. A vendor's launch email will always tell you to switch the new thing on. We'll tell you when it isn't worth it yet for your business, and why.
