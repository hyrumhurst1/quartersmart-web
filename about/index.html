The job nobody on your payroll has.

New models, agents and features ship every week, and many of them land quietly inside software you already pay for. Keeping up is a real job. In a business of 10 to 250 people, it usually belongs to nobody.

That's the job we do. We call it early adoption: using a new tool while it still gives you a head start, and skipping the ones that don't earn the time.

  1. Detect

    Launches, early-access programs, quiet product updates and credible reporting, every day.

  2. Decode

    A plain-English verdict on each one, for a business like yours: act now, prepare, wait or ignore.

  3. Deploy

    When a tool is worth it, we set it up in your accounts, test it with a few people, then teach it to the team.

  4. Stay current

    Members get Signals filtered to their own tools, with setup time held for launch weeks. When a tool changes, we adjust your setup.

Why the name

Why "Quarter."

Every shift in AI has an early group. They try the new tool first, learn it while there's still room to learn, and get the time back sooner. Think of it as the first quarter of the market to move.

QuarterSmart exists to help you move with that group on purpose, and only when the move is worth making. Plenty of launches aren't worth your time, and we'll tell you which ones.

The logo says the same thing: a circle with one quarter lifted out, a step ahead of the rest.
