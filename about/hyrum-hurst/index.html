Background

A builder who teaches the tool inside the work.

Hyrum designs AI workflows with large language models, builds end-to-end automations and internal tools, and helps small organizations put AI into their day-to-day work. He works in Claude Code every day across live client systems. His focus is turning manual, repetitive work into systems a person can check.

He founded QuarterSmart in 2025. Before AI became the whole job, he worked in direct sales and training at Skytech, where he trained and onboarded more than ten new team members. The habit stuck. A tool only counts once people use it in their own work, with their own examples.

Today he writes QuarterSmart Signals and builds the setups and automations that follow from them.
