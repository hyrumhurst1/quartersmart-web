Background

A builder who teaches the tool inside the work.

Hyrum designs AI workflows with large language models, builds automations and internal tools, and helps small organizations put AI into their day-to-day work. He works in Claude, ChatGPT and n8n every day on live client systems. His focus is turning manual, repetitive work into systems a person can check.
